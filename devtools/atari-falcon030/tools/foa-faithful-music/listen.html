Fate of Atlantis / Atari Falcon030

The complete MT-32 sound,
precomputed for Falcon playback.

Compare the same ten seconds from the game’s opening. The reference uses the actual iMUSE output, rendered offline with Munt’s MT-32 1.07 model, 32 partials and reverb.

A · Munt reference

48 kHz stereo, before conversion to the Falcon’s sample clock.

Excerpt: approximately 40–50 seconds after MIDI device initialization.

B · Falcon DMA capture

49.17 kHz stereo captured through Hatari’s Falcon DMA playback and recording path.

The WAV header rounds the hardware clock to 49,170 Hz.

The full capture matches the resampled input exactly, including both channels and the final sample.

This verifies digital transport in emulation. It is a standalone preloaded clip; disk streaming, ScummVM playback, speech mixing and physical Falcon output are still untested. Sound quality has not received a listening review.

The longer opening reference

One minute of captured iMUSE activity, followed by a shutdown release and reverb tail. The game starts cue 150, then cue 21, then overlapping cues 22, 29 and 30.

This is a Munt rendition, not a recording of a physical MT-32. The ending is imposed by the capture harness.
